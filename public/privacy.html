Privacy

comizy-dl requests the Comizy pages and image files you choose to load or download. Requests go to comizy.io and its image servers at *.cmzcdn.org.

Those services receive the requested URLs and normal connection information, including your IP address and browser request headers. Firefox can include existing cookies for the destination, as it does when using your site session. The extension adds a Comizy Referer header to its own image requests.

Series discovery opens an inactive Comizy tab and operates its chapter controls. That tab runs the site's normal scripts and may contact services chosen by the site, including advertising providers. Their behavior is controlled by Comizy and your browser settings.

Job history, selected chapters, output settings, measured image dimensions and page groupings, and download records stay in this add-on's local Firefox storage. Image processing and CBZ creation happen on your device. Removing the add-on normally removes its local records; files already saved to Downloads remain.

The extension has no analytics, telemetry, developer-operated backend, or advertising code of its own. It does not send your job history or downloaded image contents to the developer or another processing service.

Firefox's installation prompt discloses the required browsing-activity and website-content data involved in these requests. You can pause or cancel jobs, close the manager to stop scheduling more downloads, revoke site access, or remove the add-on in Firefox's Add-ons Manager.

This is an independent utility and is not affiliated with Comizy or Mozilla.
